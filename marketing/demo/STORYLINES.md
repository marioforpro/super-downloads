# Super Downloads demo — three storylines

**The one thing:** copy a video link, come back to Super Downloads, press Enter — the video lands in your editing timeline, ready to cut.
**Viewer:** an editor or creator (Mario's followers, friends, clients) who sees it on the landing, Instagram/X, or from the app.
**Real data that travels:** one real link — `youtube.com/watch?v=WhWc3b3KhnY` («Spring», Blender Studio, CC BY 4.0) — from the browser, into the app, onto the timeline.

## A · «Links become clips» — RECOMMENDED

Device: *one object across apps* · Form: *object-to-object chain* (every shot ends on the thing that opens the next).
A link in a browser lifts off as a capsule, flies into Super Downloads, becomes a download row, and the finished row is dragged out as a clip onto an editing timeline — the product's promise as one continuous journey. Then Batch mode rains three more links in, and the app icon closes it.

- **First 2 s:** black → the address bar rises out of the dark with the real link; the cursor sweeps a selection over it; giant keycaps ⌘ C press on the beat.
- **Beats:** hook (copy) → carry (⌘Tab, the capsule flies) → land (link already in the field) → Enter → download + H.264 → payoff: drag the finished video onto the timeline and it plays → Batch mode montage → seven platforms roll → end card.
- **Look:** four worlds, one palette — a light browser world, the app on the icon's own deep-blue gradient, a graphite editing world, the end card back on the icon blue; Geist display; physical UI (depth, contact shadows, 3 % grain).
- **Sound:** a 120 BPM minimal electronic bed, near-silent hook, real key clicks on the keycaps, one riser into the timeline drop, the music opening on Batch mode.

Why first: a stranger follows one link from a place they know (a browser) to the place they care about (their timeline); it is the brand's promise, not a feature list.

## B · «Copy. Switch. Enter.»

Device: *type carries the argument* · Form: *cut on the beat*. The three words fill the frame one at a time in giant kinetic type, each word cutting into a punch-in of the real UI doing it; a fourth word, «Edit.», drops the clip onto the timeline. Bold and fast (his collage/type families), but the product is seen in fragments.

## C · «The result first»

Device: *the result first* · Form: *one continuous take*. Opens on a finished clip playing in an editing timeline; the camera pulls back and rewinds through where it came from — the row, the field, the browser link — then plays forward at speed. Elegant, but the hook asks the viewer to wait for the «how».
